Speed

Release build, one core. "× real time" is how many seconds of audio render per second; model files trade speed for not needing Rust.

Stereo: before and after

The same triggers twice: before is space/width 0, the old mono sound; after is the designed width. Use headphones or speakers apart. Correlation 1.00 is mono, 0 is fully wide; cost is render time as a share of one core while the event sounds.

Sound review

Continuous sounds are rendered with a 10 s input sweep; event sounds are fired four times (full power twice, weak, distant). Then each is scored. Press play to judge it by ear; hover or tap a check for its measurement.

How to read a spectrogram here: time runs left to right, pitch bottom (30 Hz) to top (20 kHz), brightness is loudness, and the blue line is overall level. Horizontal stripes at fixed heights in a noise-like sound mean it rings like a chime; black vertical gaps mean dropouts; a glowing bottom edge means heavy sub-bass. Rebuild with tools/test_dashboard.py.
